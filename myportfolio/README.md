# My Portfolio Website

Welcome to my personal portfolio website! This site showcases my work, skills, certificates, tutorials, blog, and social media activity.

## Features

- **Home / Hero Section** – Introduce yourself with a tagline and call-to-action.
- **About Me** – Background, skills, and personal journey.
- **Projects Showcase** – Highlight your major projects with descriptions, tech stack, and links to GitHub/demo.
- **Certificates** – Display professional certifications with images or verification links.
- **Video Tutorials** – Embed or link to your tutorial videos (YouTube/Vimeo).
- **Blog Section** – Share articles, insights, and learning resources.
- **Latest Social Media Activity** – Showcase posts or updates from Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or GitHub activity.
- **Contact Section** – Contact form and links to your social profiles.
- **Skills Section** – Display tech stack icons/logos (React, Next.js, etc.).
- **Dark/Light Mode Toggle** – Modern UI preference option.
- **Responsive Design** – Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **SEO & Performance Optimized** – Fast loading and search engine friendly.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS (or your preferred styling library)
- **Hosting:** Vercel 
- **Other Tools:** Framer Motion (for animations), Formspree/Nodemailer (for contact form)
